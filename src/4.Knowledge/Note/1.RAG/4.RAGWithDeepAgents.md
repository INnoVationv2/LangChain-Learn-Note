# 基于DeepAgent的检索增强生成(RAG)

[Deep Agents](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/deepagents/overview)为 RAG 提供了基本组件：自定义检索工具、[文件系统后端](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/deepagents/backends)、[子代理](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/deepagents/subagents)、[技能](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/deepagents/skills)和[评分标准](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/deepagents/rubric)。您可以根据语料库规模、延迟要求以及答案对源数据的依赖程度，以不同的方式组合这些组件。
